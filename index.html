Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between glucose units (mmol/L and mg/dL)?

Arrow Health fully supports cross-unit display. Navigate to Profile settings in the app to switch your preferred measurement unit. The app converts all historical glucose values automatically from Hive storage.

How does the AI Meal Scanner estimate my lunch calories?

When you take a photo of your plate, the app uploads the image to our Cloud Run engine. It identifies individual food items (like vegetables, protein, grains) and queries nutritional databases to return calorie and macro breakdown estimates.

Is my medical data kept private and stored locally?

Yes. Arrow Health values privacy. All your readings (glucose readings, medicine tracking, water, growth logs) are stored locally in Hive DB boxes. If you decide to sync with the backend, your data is transferred using end-to-end SSL encryption.

How does the Medicine scanner identify my packaging?

The app uses OCR camera sweeps to extract medication name, dosage strength, and package dimensions. Once identified, you can set custom push notifications to alert you when your dose is due.

Does Arrow Health support local languages?

Yes, Arrow Health features complete localization support. You can switch between English and Bengali (বাংলা) from the settings screen, translating all dashboard cards and instructions dynamically.

How do the Growth calculators (BMR/TDEE) help me?

By entering your weight, height, age, and activity factor, Arrow Health estimates your metabolic stats. This allows you to track weight changes alongside active glucose trends to see how lifestyle changes affect your body.

Need further assistance?

If you couldn't find an answer, submit a troubleshooting ticket and our clinical support team will follow up within 24 hours.
