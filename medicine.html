About this medicine

Propranolol is a non-selective beta-adrenergic receptor blocker. It works by blocking both beta-1 and beta-2 adrenergic receptors, leading to a decrease in heart rate, reduced myocardial contractility, and lower blood pressure. It also has central nervous system effects that contribute to uses such as migraine prophylaxis and essential tremor.
